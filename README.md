<b>Kurama.Monitor</b> is built around a deliberate synthesis of distinct traditions and a strict separation of meanings.
    <b>Kūrma</b> (कूर्म) is the Sanskrit “turtle/tortoise” and Vishnu avatar associated with supporting Mount Mandara during
    the churning of the cosmic ocean. <b>Kurama</b> (九喇嘛), the Nine-Tailed Beast of <i>Naruto</i>, belongs to a different
    Japanese pop-cultural and folkloric lineage. We do <b>not</b> claim an etymological or historical identity between them.
    Their similarity of sound becomes an intentional design bridge: <b>foundation beneath transformation</b> + <b>distributed living power</b>.
